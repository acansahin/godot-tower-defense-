THE PLACE - this is the whole brief, read it before anything else:

A ROMAN CITY BURIED IN VOLCANIC ASH, seen from straight above. Not an ash field. Not a ruined
forest. A dead town drowned to its rooftops in pale grey ash: you are looking down at a street
grid that is still legible, with everything between the streets filled and smothered.

BECAUSE THE CAMERA POINTS STRAIGHT DOWN THERE IS NO VOLCANO IN THE PICTURE. Do not draw a
mountain, a cone, a horizon, a sky or a smoke plume - none of them can be seen from overhead.
The eruption shows only on the GROUND: settled ash everywhere, and one dark tongue of cooled
lava entering from the top edge of the frame.

NOTHING IS GREEN. No grass, no trees, no bushes, no moss, no ivy, no crops. Anything green is
wrong.

WHAT THE ASH LOOKS LIKE:
- The main surface is settled volcanic ash: PALE WARM GREY, dry, dusty, sunlit and clearly
  BRIGHT. This is the ground the player builds on and most of the picture is made of it. It is
  not a night scene, not a charred black waste, and not a smoking ruin.
- It is NOT featureless. Long soft wind drifts, faint ripples, and pale streaks of finer ash
  curve across it, together with the ghosted rectangles of walls buried just under the surface.
  All of this is painted FLAT INTO the ash: it is texture, not objects, and nothing in it
  stands up or casts a shadow.
- Lit EVENLY corner to corner. NO smoke drifting over the ground, NO ash cloud shadows, NO
  dark corners, NO vignette, NO orange rim-light washing across the field.

WHAT BREAKS THE ASH UP - these are the map's real features, and they are RECTILINEAR:
- CITY BLOCKS: dense masses of half-buried masonry - stubs of insula walls, tiled roof ridges
  breaking the surface, courtyards choked with ash. They are BLOCKY and SQUARE-CORNERED, laid
  out on a grid, not scattered like boulders. Several across the map, including two or three
  reaching into the middle.
- COLONNADES: straight rows of broken column stumps standing along the main streets, casting
  short real shadows.
- THE AMPHITHEATRE: one large oval bowl of stone seating half-filled with ash, at an edge or a
  corner of the picture. It is the one curved structure on the map and it should be
  unmistakable from above.
- COLLAPSE FIELDS: zones where roofs have fallen in - broken tile, snapped beams and rubble
  heaped over the ash. Two or three such zones, at the edges and in the corners.
- Keep every one of these DARKER than the open ash, so the plain is unmistakably the bright
  part of the picture.

LAVA - the map's one strongly coloured feature:
- One dark tongue of cooled lava enters at the TOP edge, and one or two NARROW channels of

- They must not touch the road, must not cross it, must not run through the open ash beside

  nothing standing in them at all.
- Every stretch of the road must have such an open pocket beside it within a short distance. A
  street walled in by standing masonry on both sides is a stretch nobody can defend.
- Inside those pockets: no columns, no rubble, no wall stubs, no fallen tile, no debris. Flat
  ash texture only.
